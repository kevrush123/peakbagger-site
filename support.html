Peak Bagger Support

Something not working, or an idea for making Peak Bagger better? Email us and we'll get back to you. Peak Bagger is made by one person in the UK, so replies can take a few days.

Report a problem Suggest an idea

You can also email support@peakbaggerapp.com directly. In the app, open the credits (tap the © OpenStreetMap contributors note on the map) and choose Report a problem or Suggest an idea; it fills in your app version and phone for you.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please tell us your phone model, whether it is the iPhone or Android app, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

  • Delete your account: open the Friends tab and tap Delete my account. This removes your account and everything stored online.
  • Scotland and the rest of the UK map, and Munro photos: these download the first time you need them. To use them without signal, open Your progress and tap Download all before you go.
  • Friends and walks: add a friend with their friend code (Friends tab). When you log a walk, tick who was with you and the summits count on their tally too.
  • Location: Peak Bagger uses your location to show you on the map, follow a walk and tick off summits. It records in the background only while you are recording a walk.
  • Signing in: we email you a sign-in link. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder.
