Everything for the day on the hill

From choosing a walk to ticking off the summit.

Maps

LIDAR-scanned maps

An optional 1 m LIDAR map of the Lake District shows every crag, gill and ridge, with height-labelled contours across the Lakes and Scotland.

Routes

Real footpaths, not straight lines

Rounds are plotted along OpenStreetMap paths from the car park and back, and avoid paths that don't exist on the ground.

Navigate

Lock screen navigation

A compass and the distance to the next turn on your lock screen, so you never need to take your gloves off on a cold day to see where you're going.

Plan

Find the right walk fast

Search by time, difficulty and length, ranked by the summits you still need. See distance, climb and a height profile before you go.

Weather

Summit weather first

Hour-by-hour forecast at the height of the top, with wind, gusts and freezing level.

Getting there

Parking and directions

See where to park and open driving directions to the start in one tap.

Track

Tick off every summit

Record walks, get a "bagged!" alert as you reach the top, and watch your tally climb with badges along the way.

Share

Share with friends

Tag friends on walks, compare tallies and share each summit as a card.

Help and support

Questions, problems or ideas? See the support page or email support@peakbaggerapp.com and say which phone you use.

  • Delete your account: open the Friends tab and tap Delete my account. This removes your account and everything stored online.
  • Scotland map and Munro photos: these download the first time you need them. To use them without signal, open Your progress and tap Download all before you go.
  • Location: Peak Bagger uses your location to show you on the map, follow a walk and tick off summits. It records in the background only while you're recording a walk.
