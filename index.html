Help and support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@peakbaggerapp.com and say which phone you use.

  • Delete your account: open the Friends tab and tap Delete my account. This removes your account and everything stored online.
  • Scotland map and Munro photos: these download the first time you need them. To use them without signal, open Your progress and tap Download all before you go.
  • Location: Peak Bagger uses your location to show you on the map, follow a walk and tick off summits. It records in the background only while you're recording a walk.
