Delete your Peak Bagger account

Peak Bagger works without an account. If you made one and want it gone, you can delete it yourself in the app, or ask us to do it.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Peak Bagger and go to the Friends tab (you need to be signed in).
  2. Tap Account, then Delete my account, and confirm.

Deleting is permanent. It removes your account and everything stored on our servers: your email address, display name, ticks, walks (including saved routes), summit notes, friend connections and tally.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, email support@peakbaggerapp.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Peak Bagger account". We'll delete it and reply to confirm, normally within 7 days.

What stays

Anything stored only on your phone (such as photos in your journal) is not on our servers. Delete the app or use Remove downloads in Your progress to clear it. We keep no backup of deleted accounts.
